About VYOMA

Smart navigation, even when GNSS is unavailable.

VYOMA is an intelligent navigation prototype that combines smartphone GNSS, accelerometer and gyroscope data with AI-assisted dead reckoning.

AI + Dead ReckoningEstimates movement during GNSS interruptions.
Smart Route GuidanceProvides route tracking, map interaction and navigation status.

VYOMA is a student-developed prototype. Real-world accuracy requires further vehicle testing and validation.
